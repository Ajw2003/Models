# Roadmap

What done means for this repo: each model is built from a committed script, reviewed against its
concept, and, where it is a character, rigged and animated.

## Milestone 1 — Models moved in (20%)

**Contains:** the goose and scythe with their history, scripts and review sheets.
**Acceptance:** every file matches the original branch byte for byte, and `main` holds them.

## Milestone 2 — Goose boss rig (20%)

**Contains:** the 59-bone generic rig bound to the pass 19 goose mesh.
**Acceptance:** a posed review sheet shows the necks, wings and legs bending without tearing, and
the look is recorded.

## Milestone 3 — Goose boss clips (50%)

**Contains:** the 13 clips decided on 2026-10-21 (see `docs/6-decisions/Decisions.md`), an
animation-only FBX and a clip manifest.
**Acceptance:** every clip has 8 stills and an MP4 on the real model, feet do not slide while
planted, nothing goes below the floor, and aj has checked them in a pull request.
